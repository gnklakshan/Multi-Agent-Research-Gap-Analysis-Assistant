# Common Research Gaps
## Privacy Concerns & Technical Details
Several papers highlight the importance of privacy in fall detection systems, particularly through techniques like federated learning and visual obfuscation. However, a significant gap exists in the depth of discussion regarding the *actual* privacy guarantees offered by these methods. Specifically, the effectiveness of Gaussian-based visual obfuscation needs rigorous quantification, and the potential vulnerabilities associated with it (e.g., differential privacy, re-identification risks) are not fully addressed.  Furthermore, the reliance on datasets like UP-Fall raises questions about generalizability and the potential for bias.

- Strength of evidence: High
- Why it matters: Without a thorough understanding of the privacy risks and limitations of these techniques, the deployment of fall detection systems could inadvertently compromise the privacy of vulnerable individuals.  Furthermore, reliance on specific datasets can lead to biased or inaccurate results.
- Opportunity: Investigating the effectiveness of different privacy-enhancing technologies (PETs) for fall detection, including robust differential privacy mechanisms and techniques for mitigating re-identification risks.
- Papers: 1, 3, 4, 5
